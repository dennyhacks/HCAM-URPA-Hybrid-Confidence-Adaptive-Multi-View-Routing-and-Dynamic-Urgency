The Proposed Solution: HCAM-URPA Architecture

Hybrid Confidence-Adaptive Multi-View Routing + Urgency Propagation Algorithm

🔷 Pillar 1: HCAM (Multi-View Routing)

Combines 3 complementary, syllabus-compliant views:

  • Text View ($p_T$): TF-IDF bi-grams + Multinomial Logistic Regression.
  • Context View ($p_C$): Telemetry + 8 photo features via CART Decision Tree.
  • Retrieval View ($p_R$): Cosine-similarity KNN Recommender (Module 5).
c_v = 1 - H(p_v) / ln(K) [Shannon Confidence]
w_v = (α_v · c_v) / ∑ (α_u · c_u) [Dynamic Weights]
p_final = ∑ w_v · p_v

• Photo-Aware Reliability (α_v): Computed separately for photo-present (α_C = 0.959) vs photo-absent (α_C = 0.052), naturally collapsing Context View when photos are missing.

⚡ Pillar 2: URPA (Urgency Propagation)

Decouples static physical severity from dynamic dispatch urgency:

  • Step 1: Multiple Linear Regression predicts base structural damage score $s \in [0, 1]$.
  • Step 2: Nonlinear hazard differential equation fitted via scipy.optimize.curve_fit:
hazard = ā · exp(-D / τ) + b · w
U = s + (1 - s) · (1 - exp(-hazard))

• Guaranteed Physical Invariant: Because $hazard \ge 0$, $(1 - e^{-hazard}) \ge 0$, mathematically proving $U \ge s$ at all times! Linear regression violates this rule.

● Module 3: Statistical Inference & Hypothesis Testing

Grounding the Problem with Formal Statistical Testing

Validating population hypotheses and operational invariants prior to modeling

Test 1 One-Way ANOVA: Severity across Departments

$H_0$: Mean physical severity is identical across all 5 railway departments.

$H_1$: At least one department has significantly different damage severity.

F = 926.83, p = 0.0000
Decision: Reject H0 (p < 0.05)

Plain-English Conclusion: Confirms Mechanical (0.75) and Security (0.83) carry fundamentally higher structural severity than Cleaning (0.35).

Test 2 Two-Sample t-Test: Urgency vs Photo Presence

$H_0$: Mean urgency is identical whether a photo is attached or not.

$H_1$: Uploading a photo significantly alters operational urgency.

t = 0.957, p = 0.3385
Decision: Fail to Reject H0 (p ≥ 0.05)

Plain-English Conclusion: Proves operational urgency is driven by yard proximity and passenger wait time, NOT simply whether a photo was uploaded.

Test 3 Chi-Square Test: Department vs Station Code

$H_0$: Grievance department type is independent of station code.

χ² = 59.88, p = 0.4433
Decision: Fail to Reject H0 (p ≥ 0.05)

Conclusion: Complaint distribution is uniform across Indian stations; no station has an artificial bias toward a single department.

Test 4 95% Confidence Interval for Urgency

Computing margin of error using Student's t-distribution:

[0.6766 , 0.6869]
95% Confidence Interval for Mean Urgency

Interpretation: We are 95% confident that the true population mean urgency for Indian Railways lies between 0.677 and 0.687.
